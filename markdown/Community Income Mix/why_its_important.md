The *Update:* *Connections 2050* Long-Range Plan seeks to support communities where people of different income levels have access to similar opportunities and resources across the region. A broader mix of household incomes has been shown to improve quality of life overall. When income levels are more evenly distributed across communities, there are often gains in essential resources like public education, green space, and infrastructure. Children in these communities are also more likely to have access to a wider range of opportunities that support long-term success.
