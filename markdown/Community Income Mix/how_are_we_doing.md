The region has seen only modest change in how households are distributed by income across communities over time. While there has been a slight improvement in recent years, overall patterns remain largely consistent with past decades.

The Income Concentration Index indicates that, as of 2024, about 40% of residents would need to relocate for households to be evenly distributed by income across communities. This reflects only a small change since 1990, when the Index was at 41.1%. Recent trends point to gradual movement toward a more balanced distribution, but progress has been limited. Research has also found a [slight trend in decreasing concentrations of poverty](https://penniur.upenn.edu/publications/ten-ways-covid-19-changed-americas-cities-or-maybe-not#poverty-reductions-surprising-progress-but-will-it-stick) in the nation’s urban areas since the pandemic.
