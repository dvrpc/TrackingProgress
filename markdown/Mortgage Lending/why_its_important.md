Homeownership remains a key mechanism for households to gain and build wealth, and pass it on to their children—referred to as generational wealth. However, past policies made it disproportionately difficult for some populations to access the capital needed to purchase a home, the ramifications of which persist today. Access to loans is critical to the economic vitality and resiliency of the Greater Philadelphia region and all of its residents. This criterion aligns with the *Update: Connections 2050 Plan* goal to foster socioeconomically integrated neighborhoods.
