Total decisioned applications (originations plus denials) in the region for all purposes have declined by 80%, from 649,902 in 2007 to just 128,387 in 2024. The initial decline occurred as a result of the Great Recession. Continued declines over the last decade or so may represent the general lack of affordability within the housing market, changes to regulatory requirements, and/or shifts in credit access. Interest rates increased starting in 2021—and remain elevated compared to pandemic-era lows—further impacting mortgage activity. 

However, the region’s loan origination rate for all purposes has increased by about 12 percentage points, from 65.0% in 2007 to 77.1% in 2024. The rate generally increased between the Great Recession in 2008 to the COVID pandemic to a peak of 83.5% in 2020. It has generally declined since then.
