Regionally, mortgage loan origination rates have been highest overall for white individuals over this data series. This group had 81.5% of loan applications issued in 2024. Similarly, males and non-Latinx individuals have had a higher loan origination rate than females and Latinx individuals since 2007. However, these gaps have generally narrowed by 8% to 10% for most groups since 2007, although progress has largely stalled since 2021. One notable exception to this trend is the gap between Asian and white, which was just 1.7% in 2007 and was 0.8% in 2024. The Black-to-white gap remains the highest in this dataset, at 18.1% in 2024. 

Total loan application rates have declined by more than 80%, from nearly 650,000 in 2007 to just 128,000 in 2024. The initial decline occurred as a result of the Great Recession. Continued declines over the last decade or so may represent the general lack of affordability within the housing market and/or the increased availability of alternative financing options for home improvement projects, while rising interest rates starting in 2021 further reduced mortgage activity.
