Mortgage lending information comes from the Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council's (FFIEC) Home Mortgage Disclosure Act (HMDA) data. *Tracking Progress* includes data on *loan originations*—the creation of a loan after bank approval to purchase a home, undertake home improvements, or refinance an existing mortgage—and *application denials*—when a financial institution decides not to make a loan to an applicant for these same purposes. Together, these are considered *decisioned applications*. 

The first chart shows the regional loan origination rate over time. Total decisioned applications for each year are shown in the second chart, which shows the volume of attempted loan activity occurring within the region. Loan origination rates are calculated from the number of loan applications that are approved (*originated*) divided by the total applications submitted (*decisioned*). The loan’s purpose can be selected via a right dropdown list in both charts.

HMDA data includes six other actions that aren’t included in Tracking Progress for when an applicant withdraws their application or does not accept the approved loan, the file is closed due to incomplete information, a loan is purchased by another institution, or a pre-approval request is denied or approved but not accepted. This data does not include those who can pay cash for home purchases or home improvements, and thus do not need to make a mortgage loan application.
