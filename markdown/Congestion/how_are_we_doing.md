The congestion data show that reliability and congestion are worsening overall in the region. Planning Time Index (PTI) on highways reached a new peak at 1.35 in 2025 (Chart 1), and is up 12% since 2021. Peak-hours of excessive delay per capita reached new peaks as it increased by 21% in the Philadelphia Urban Area and 39% in the Trenton Urban Area between 2024 and 2025 (Chart 4). 

However, congestion and reliability issues are not evenly spread across the region, time periods, and facility types. PTI on local roads remains near its pandemic low. The total number of Interstate segments with reliable travel conditions has increased in recent years (Chart 2), suggesting congestion issues are concentrated in just a handful of locations. Truck Travel Time Reliability (Chart 3), which measures PTI on the region’s Interstates, continues to rise above the 2020 pandemic low (1.55). It reached 1.75 in 2025, although it remains well below the 2018 peak of 1.91. 

Travel is worsening during mid-day periods, and congestion is highest and roads are least reliable in Philadelphia. These issues don’t just impact drivers, as Chart 5 shows reliability is also decreasing for the region’s bus riders.
