Inflation-adjusted household incomes are up by 4% since 2006. However, between 2006 and 2024, the gap in annual income change between the highest income households (fifth-quintile) and lowest-income households (first quintile) has widened by about 16%. Fifth-quintile households have seen their incomes grow by 14%, an increase of $40,000 per year. First-quintile households have experienced a 2% decline in income, or $400 less annually. The fifth-quintile households' earnings were 13 times higher in 2006 and 15.6 times higher in 2024 than first-quintile households’.
