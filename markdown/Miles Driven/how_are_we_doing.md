There were 108.5 million daily vehicle miles traveled (VMT) in the region in 2024, a 3.8% decrease from 2023. The region’s Pennsylvania counties experienced a 6.8% decrease in daily VMT, from 69.0 million in 2023 to 64.3 million in 2024, while the region's New Jersey counties experienced a slight increase (0.8%) in daily VMT, from 43.8 million in 2023 to 44.2 million in 2024. Montgomery County had the most daily VMT in the region at 16.9 million in 2024, while Delaware County had the least at 8.6 million. Daily VMT declined during the Great Recession, then increased as the economy improved from 2012 to 2019. The onset of the COVID-19 pandemic caused an 18% drop in miles driven in 2020. This was immediately followed by a 20% jump in daily VMT, the largest one-year gain since 2000. Daily VMT per capita helps conceptualize individuals’ driving habits. This has generally increased in the years since the pandemic, though it remains 10% below the 2006 peak. In 2024, Burlington (28.7 daily miles per capita) and Gloucester counties (28.3) led the region in daily VMT per capita, while Philadelphia had the lowest at 9.7.

The number of personal vehicles available to the region’s households grew by 790,000 from 2000 to 2024, a 26% increase. Per capita vehicle ownership also increased 14%, from 0.56 cars per person in 2000 to 0.64 in 2024. Philadelphia residents had the lowest car ownership rate in 2024, with less than one vehicle for every two people, though it has grown the fastest: from 0.36 to 0.46 vehicles per capita, or 28%, since 2000. Bucks, Burlington, Chester, Gloucester, and Montgomery counties all had more than 0.7 vehicles per person in 2024.
