Business formations are an important indicator of the resilience and entrepreneurial spirit of the local economy. Uncertain times present opportunities and necessitate adaptation to changing economic conditions. New businesses create jobs that shape the prosperity of households and the region at large. The *Update: Connections 2050* Plan seeks to build an innovative and connected economy with opportunity and shared prosperity, develop a well-trained and adaptable regional workforce, and support a variety of regional economic sectors. Business formation statistics help quantify where we stand in achieving these goals.
