Labor force participation rate is a measure of the active workforce. The chart shows the percentage of participation for residents between 16 and 64 years old in each geography. It shows the total number of noninstitutionalized people in that age range who are employed or actively seeking employment, divided by the total noninstitutionalized population in that age range.

Planning areas are made up of minor civil divisions (MCDs), which only have ACS 5-year labor participation rate data available for them, due to the sampling issues that arise with having ACS 1-year data at small geographic levels. This leads to 2005 being the earliest year of data available for the counties, and 2009 being the earliest year of data available for the MCDs, since 2009 is the earliest year data can be averaged with its preceding four years (2005-2009).

*Note: The Census Bureau issued [a statement acknowledging errors in 2017 ACS data for Philadelphia County](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/technical-documentation/errata/121.html). Philadelphia estimates for geography of residence are omitted as a result, but the error may impact the regional aggregations. Also note, the 2020 one-year ACS was not released due to disruptions of the COVID-19 pandemic.*
