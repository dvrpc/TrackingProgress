Greater Philadelphia’s labor force participation rate reached 78.8% in 2024, its highest since 2005 and up 2.8 percentage points over that period. In 2024, two-thirds of counties, along with all suburban county groupings and planning areas, either reached their highest rates since 2005 or were within 0.1 percentage points of their highest level. Although the participation rate varied from 2005 to 2018, the region’s rate has increased every year that data was released since 2018. In 2024, the Core Cities had the region’s largest growth in participation when compared to the 2005 figures, led by an increase of 7.6% in Philadelphia’s over that period.
