A productive and engaged labor force is essential for the region’s economic vitality. Labor force is a measure of who is employed or actively engaged in seeking employment. A higher labor force participation rate indicates that a larger share of the population is engaged in the labor market. While this can be associated with strong labor demand or attractive wages, it may also reflect other factors such as demographic changes or broader economic conditions. Lower participation rates can indicate that people are discouraged from finding work and/or they are unable to work due to health, family caregiving, or other factors. Individuals, families, and communities must have access to employment opportunities to build skills, generate wealth, and participate in the local economy. 

This indicator relates to the *Update: Connections 2050* Plan vision for an innovative and connected economy with opportunity and shared prosperity.
