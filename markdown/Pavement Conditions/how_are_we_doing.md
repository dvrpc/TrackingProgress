In 2023, 4.2% of the region’s NHS pavement lane miles were in poor condition, according to the PM2 metric. Poor condition pavement in terms of the IRI declined by 6% regionally from its 2015 peak of 31.8%, but is still 1.1% higher than the 2010 base year figure of 24.7%. Non-NHS roadways are in much worse shape than NHS roadways by IRI, where in 2023, 32% of non-NHS pavement segment miles were in poor condition, versus 23.4% of non-interstate NHS pavement segment miles, and just 7.6% of NHS interstate pavement segment miles.

In Pennsylvania, poor condition pavement by IRI increased from 25.5% in 2010 to 36.8% in 2015, and declined to 31.3% in 2023. 1,292.4 Pennsylvania segment miles were in poor condition in 2023 according to the same metric; a 20.6% increase from 2010. In New Jersey, poor condition pavement dropped from 22.6% of segment miles to 12.6% between 2010 and 2023 by IRI. Most counties track fairly closely with others in their state. However, in Philadelphia, poor conditions were experienced on at least half their pavement segment miles according to IRI for almost all years between 2017 and 2023.
